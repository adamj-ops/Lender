## Key Notes

1. **Predictable Timing**: All payments on 1st of month, shown prominently

2. **Full Transparency**: Every payment broken down by note and calculation

3. **Secure Banking**: Bank updates require verification delay for security

4. **Clear History**: Searchable, filterable payment history with statuses

5. **Forward Looking**: Payment schedule shows expected future payments
