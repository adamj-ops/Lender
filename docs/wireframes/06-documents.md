## Key Notes

1. **Secure Vault**: All documents encrypted at rest and in transit

2. **Auto-Generation**: Monthly statements auto-generated on 1st of month

3. **Compliance Tracking**: System tracks expiration dates and sends reminders

4. **Tax Ready**: 1099-INT available by January 31 each year

5. **Audit Trail**: All document access logged for compliance
